Travel insurance for a trip abroad — বিদেশ ভ্রমণে বীমা

Travel insurance is a policy you can buy for a specific trip, for the travellers and dates you enter. From here you can continue to our partner EKTA and check the policy options shown for your destination and travel dates yourself. We do not publish invented insurance prices on this page, and we do not promise that any particular cost or situation will be covered — the policy terms, coverage, exclusions, limits and final price are shown by EKTA before you pay.

Check travel insurance on EKTA

Who may consider it — কার জন্য দরকার হতে পারে

  • Bangladeshi travellers going abroad for tourism, family visits, study or work who want to check what a trip policy would cover before they fly.
  • Travellers whose destination, visa or tour requires insurance — check the exact requirement for your case, then check on EKTA whether a policy matches it.
  • Anyone comparing policies for a single trip — enter the real destination, dates and travellers on EKTA and read the policy shown for those details.

How it works (3 steps) — কীভাবে কাজ করে

  1. Click the EKTA button above. You leave Cheap Ticket Finder and open the partner site in a new tab.
  2. On EKTA, enter your destination, travel dates and travellers, then read the policy terms for the option shown.
  3. Buy on the partner site only if the terms suit your trip. We do not take payment, and we never ask for passport, card or booking-login details.

Before you pay — checks

  • Check the policy terms and coverage on the EKTA page before buying: what is covered, what is excluded, and any limits or excess that apply.
  • Check the destination, start and end dates, and every traveller name and detail you entered — they must match your actual trip and documents.
  • Check how pre-existing medical conditions, planned activities, trip changes and cancellations are treated in the policy wording, and how to make a claim.
  • Compare the final payable total on EKTA before you pay, and keep the policy documents EKTA issues after purchase.
