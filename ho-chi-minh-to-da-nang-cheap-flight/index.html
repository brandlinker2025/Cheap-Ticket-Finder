Separate-ticket & transit/entry caution

Two tickets = two contracts. If the first flight is late, the second airline is not obliged to rebook you free and the second fare can be lost as a no-show. With checked bags on separate tickets you may need immigration → baggage reclaim → check-in again, so keep a large buffer; arriving the night before the domestic leg is our safer advice (guidance, not an official airline rule).

Separate tickets can mean entering Vietnam. Do not assume airside transit or any visa/e-visa eligibility on separate bookings.

No verified official Vietnam entry link is provided in our research for this page, so none is guessed here. Check official Vietnamese immigration/government sources and the airline before booking.

Cheap low-cost fares are often non-refundable and change fees can be high — read the fare rules before paying.
