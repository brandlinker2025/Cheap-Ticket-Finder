🚕 Airport Transfer

Last updated: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka)

Airport to hotel, booked ahead

An airport transfer is a pre-booked car that meets you at the airport and takes you to your hotel or address. From here you can continue to our partner KiwiTaxi and check a transfer for your airport, date and passengers yourself. We do not publish invented transfer prices on this page — the real vehicle options and final price for your route are shown by KiwiTaxi before you pay.

Who needs it

  • Families, groups and anyone arriving late at night or with heavy baggage who wants the airport-to-hotel ride sorted before flying.
  • First-time visitors to cities such as Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore or Dubai who prefer a driver waiting at arrivals over negotiating transport after landing.
  • Travellers who want to compare a pre-booked transfer against local taxi or ride apps on total price, vehicle size and cancellation terms.

How it works (3 steps)

  1. Click the KiwiTaxi button above. You leave Cheap Ticket Finder and open the partner site in a new tab.
  2. On KiwiTaxi, enter your arrival airport, hotel or address, date, flight time and passenger count, then compare vehicle options yourself.
  3. Book and pay on the partner site only. We do not take payment, and we never ask for passport, card or booking-login details.

Before you pay — checks

  • Compare the final payable total and check what is included: meet-and-greet, flight-delay waiting, tolls, child seats and extra baggage.
  • Confirm pickup instructions, driver contact process, free waiting time if your flight is delayed, and the cancellation policy.
  • Use your correct flight number and arrival time so the driver can track delays, and keep enough time after landing for immigration and baggage.

After you land

An airport transfer is a car arranged before you fly, from the airport to a hotel or another address. It is separate from the flight ticket. The driver meets you under the partner’s instructions, which you should read before you travel. Some meetings are in the arrivals hall. Some use a named meeting point.

Put the flight number and landing time on the booking so a delay can be seen. Immigration and baggage still take time after the aircraft parks. Check the free waiting time on the partner page, and check what happens if the flight is early or cancelled.

Compare the vehicle size with your group and your bags. A car that is too small is a problem you only see at the curb. Child seats, tolls, and extra stops are stated on the partner page when they are offered. Confirm the final total there. This site does not dispatch cars and does not take the payment.
