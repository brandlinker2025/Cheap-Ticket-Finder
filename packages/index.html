How it works (3 steps)

  1. Click a partner button above. You leave Cheap Ticket Finder and open the partner site in a new tab.
  2. On the partner site, search your destination and date, and read the full itinerary for that exact package or activity.
  3. Book and pay on the partner site only. We do not take payment, and we never ask for passport, card or booking-login details.

Before you pay — package checks

  • Check what is included and excluded: transport, guide, entry tickets, meals, hotel pickup and personal expenses.
  • Confirm meeting point / pickup time, group or private format, language, duration and the cancellation / reschedule policy.
  • Compare the final payable total with taxes and fees, and check whether your flight arrival and baggage leave enough time before the tour starts.
  • A package activity is separate from your flight ticket unless the partner page clearly says otherwise — do not assume flights or hotels are included.
