London is the main long-haul route for the British-Bangladeshi community and students. One-stop fares via Gulf or Turkish hubs often compete with non-stop flights. Use this guide to check your exact dates — no invented live price is shown here.

How to find the cheapest dates (3 steps)

  1. Open the Cheap Ticket Finder search tool, set From = Dhaka and To = London, and pick flexible dates on the calendar.
  2. Open the on-site flight search and compare prices from partner sites. The final price is confirmed on the booking site.
  3. Before paying, compare the total with baggage, seat and payment fees added. The lowest headline fare is not always the lowest total.

Route tips — Dhaka to London

  • Search all London airports together (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton) — the search tool does this for London.
  • Students: look for fare families with extra baggage and flexible changes.
  • December and summer holidays are peak — compare several weeks if your dates are flexible.

Airlines on this route

Biman Bangladesh operates non-stop flights to London Heathrow. One-stop options via Dubai, Doha, Istanbul and other hubs are widely available — compare total time and baggage. Schedules change, so always check the search for your dates.

Baggage & total-cost warning

Checked baggage, cabin baggage, seat selection, meals and change/refund rules depend on the airline and the exact fare family. Always confirm the allowance on the airline or provider page for the fare you are about to buy. Compare the final payable total, not a base fare example. Nothing on this page is a live fare.

Visa & entry check

Confirm UK visa and passport validity from official UK government sources before booking. Transit through a hub country may need its own transit rules. This guide does not give visa advice.

FAQ

What is the Dhaka to London ticket price today?

It depends on your travel dates, airline and fare family, so we do not print a fixed price here. Open the search tool, pick your dates, and compare prices from partner sites in the on-site flight search.

Does Cheap Ticket Finder sell tickets?

No. We build the dated search; booking and payment happen with the airline/provider.

How can I lower the total cost?

Stay flexible with dates, compare the total including baggage, and check both non-stop and connecting options. No specific day guarantees the lowest fare.
