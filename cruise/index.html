Ha Long Bay overnight cruise, Vietnam

হা লং বে-তে এক রাতের ক্রুজ, ভিয়েতনাম

UNESCO World Heritage bay of limestone islands. 2-day/1-night boats include a cabin, meals, kayaking and cave visits; most offer pickup from Hanoi.

Cheapest authentic way: Compare reviewed overnight cruises on Viator (free cancellation on many), or book an operator such as Ambassador Cruise directly.

Beaches & Boat Activities

সৈকত ও নৌকা ভ্রমণ — আন্দামান সাগর (ফুকেট, ফি ফি, ক্রাবি, সিমিলান) আর মালয়েশিয়ার লংকাউই: কী করবেন আর কোন নৌকা বা ফেরিতে যাবেন।

Phuket — beaches and island boat trips

ফুকেট — সৈকত আর দ্বীপে নৌকা ভ্রমণ

Patong, Kata and Karon beaches; day boats to Phang Nga Bay, James Bond Island and the Phi Phi Islands; snorkelling and sunset cruises.

Cheapest authentic way: Book day cruises and speedboat trips on Viator; fly Dhaka–Phuket via Bangkok or Kuala Lumpur.

Phi Phi Islands & Maya Bay — speedboat day trip

ফি ফি দ্বীপ ও মায়া বে — স্পিডবোটে একদিনের ভ্রমণ

Krabi — Railay, Ao Nang and the 4 Islands

ক্রাবি — রাইলে, আও নাং আর ৪ দ্বীপ

Rock climbing at Railay, kayaking in mangroves, longtail boats from Ao Nang, Thale Waek sandbar, Koh Hong and Koh Poda.

Similan Islands — Andaman Sea snorkelling (seasonal)

সিমিলান দ্বীপপুঞ্জ — আন্দামান সাগরে স্নরকেলিং (মৌসুমি)

Mu Ko Similan National Park opens 15 October to 15 May only. Speedboats leave from Khao Lak and Phuket; a national park fee is paid separately.

Cheapest authentic way: Speedboat day tour from Phuket on Viator; check the park is open on your date.

Langkawi, Malaysia — island hopping and ferries

লংকাউই, মালয়েশিয়া — দ্বীপ ঘোরা আর ফেরি

Island hopping to Dayang Bunting (Pregnant Maiden Lake), eagle feeding at Singa Besar and the beach at Beras Basah. Ferries connect Langkawi with Kuala Perlis, Kuala Kedah and Penang.

Cheapest authentic way: Island hopping on Viator; ferry tickets direct from Langkawi Ferry Line. Fly Dhaka–Kuala Lumpur–Langkawi.

Before you book

  • Check the visa rules for Singapore, India, Sri Lanka or the Maldives for your passport before you pay.
  • Book your flight to the port so you arrive at least a day before sailing. Search flights.
  • Read what the cruise fare includes: taxes, port fees, gratuities and drinks can be extra.
