Before you book

  • Check the visa rules for Singapore, India, Sri Lanka or the Maldives for your passport before you pay.
  • Book your flight to the port so you arrive at least a day before sailing. Search flights.
  • Read what the cruise fare includes: taxes, port fees, gratuities and drinks can be extra.
