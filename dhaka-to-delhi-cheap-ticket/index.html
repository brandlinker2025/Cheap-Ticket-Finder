How to find the cheapest dates (3 steps)

  1. Open the Cheap Ticket Finder search tool, set From = Dhaka and To = Delhi, and pick flexible dates on the calendar.
  2. Open the on-site flight search and compare prices from partner sites. The final price is confirmed on the booking site.
  3. Before paying, compare the total with baggage, seat and payment fees added. The lowest headline fare is not always the lowest total.

Route tips — Dhaka to Delhi

  • Medical travellers: pick flights with enough buffer before hospital appointments.
  • Compare the direct fare with DAC–CCU + CCU–DEL on one ticket.
  • Low-cost fares often include only cabin baggage — add checked bags before comparing totals.

Airlines on this route

IndiGo and Air India have operated Dhaka to Delhi flights. Connecting itineraries via Kolkata are also common, so compare total time and baggage. Schedules change, so always check the search for your dates.

Baggage & total-cost warning

Checked baggage, cabin baggage, seat selection, meals and change/refund rules depend on the airline and the exact fare family. Always confirm the allowance on the airline or provider page for the fare you are about to buy. Compare the final payable total, not a base fare example. Nothing on this page is a live fare.

Visa & entry check

Confirm India visa type (tourist or medical) and passport validity from official sources before booking. This guide does not give visa advice.
