About Cheap Ticket Finder

Bangladesh-based travellers der jonno lower-cost flight khuje pawar ekta simple search helper.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What we do

Cheap Ticket Finder helps Bangladesh-based travellers find lower-cost flights. You pick From / To and dates, and we build a dated Google Flights (tfs) search for you, with official airline booking links as a secondary option.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell tickets.
  • We do not invent fares (৳). We never print a made-up price.
  • We do not take payment, and we do not ask for passport, card, or booking-login details.

Where the real ticket comes from

The final price is shown by the provider. Book with the airline on Google Flights or on the official airline site — that is the authentic, airline-issued ticket. Official airline sites may open a higher fare family (such as Flex), which is why they stay secondary.

Who runs this site

Operator: Cheap Ticket Finder BD. No address or licence is claimed here.
