About Cheap Ticket Finder

A simple search helper for Bangladesh-based travellers looking for lower-cost flights.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What we do

Cheap Ticket Finder helps Bangladesh-based travellers find lower-cost flights. You pick From / To and dates, then search flights and compare prices from partner sites in the on-site flight search and Travelpayouts results, with official airline booking links as a secondary option.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell tickets.
  • We do not invent fares. We never print a made-up price.
  • We do not take payment, and we do not ask for passport, card, or booking-login details.

Where the real ticket comes from

The final price is shown by the provider. Book from the on-site flight search or on the official airline site — that is the authentic, airline-issued ticket. Official airline sites may open a higher fare family (such as Flex), which is why they stay secondary.

Who runs this site

Cheap Ticket Finder is a venture of BrandLinker, an independent company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, founded by RS Lina (tagline: Brands Connect Opportunities). BrandLinker builds and runs this site, checks the routes and fares we publish, and keeps the airline and partner links up to date.

BrandLinker is not an airline or a travel agency and does not sell tickets. Some links are affiliate links: if you book, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you (see Disclosure). No address or licence is claimed here. Questions or a wrong link? Contact us.
