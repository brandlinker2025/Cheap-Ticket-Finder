Dhaka to Kuala Lumpur Cheap Ticket

DAC → KUL · ঢাকা টু কুয়ালালামপুর — how to check the cheapest real fare for your dates. Last updated: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). No live fare is published on this page.

Kuala Lumpur fares vary a lot between low-cost and full-service airlines, mainly because checked baggage, meals and seat selection may be separate. Compare the final total on Google Flights for your dates — no live fare is printed here.

How to find the cheapest dates (3 steps)

  1. Open the Cheap Ticket Finder search tool, set From / To to this route and pick flexible dates on the calendar.
  2. Use the primary Open Google Flights button. Google Flights / the airline there shows the real, current total price — that is where booking happens.
  3. Before paying, compare the total with baggage, seat and payment fees added. The lowest headline fare is not always the lowest total.

Route tips — Dhaka to Kuala Lumpur

  • On low-cost options, add checked baggage before comparing — a 'cheap' base fare can stop being cheap after bags.
  • Compare arrival time and airport transfer cost into the city, not only the fare.
  • Try a few date combinations; shifting by 1–3 days can change the available fare families.

Baggage & total-cost warning

Checked baggage, cabin baggage, seat selection, meals and change/refund rules depend on the airline and the exact fare family. Always confirm the allowance in kg/pieces on the airline or provider page for the fare you are about to buy. Compare the final payable total, not a base fare example. Any price example used while learning the tool is only an example of how to read the screen — it is not a live fare and is not a quote for this route.

Transit & booking-condition check

Check whether your option is non-stop or connecting, and confirm Malaysia entry requirements from official sources before booking. Rules change, so do not rely on a guide page for visa decisions.
