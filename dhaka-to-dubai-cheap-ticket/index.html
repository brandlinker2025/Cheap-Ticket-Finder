Dhaka to Dubai Cheap Ticket

DAC → DXB (also check SHJ options if shown) · ঢাকা টু দুবাই — how to check the cheapest real fare for your dates. Last updated: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). No live fare is published on this page.

Dubai options can include non-stop and connecting flights with very different total travel times. A lower fare with a long layover is not automatically better value. Check your dates and the full journey on Google Flights — this page prints no live fare.

How to find the cheapest dates (3 steps)

  1. Open the Cheap Ticket Finder search tool, set From / To to this route and pick flexible dates on the calendar.
  2. Use the primary Open Google Flights button. Google Flights / the airline there shows the real, current total price — that is where booking happens.
  3. Before paying, compare the total with baggage, seat and payment fees added. The lowest headline fare is not always the lowest total.

Route tips — Dhaka to Dubai

  • Compare total travel time, not just price: a cheap connection can add many hours.
  • Check if a nearby-airport option (for example Sharjah, if shown for your search) really saves money after ground transfer.
  • Confirm checked baggage in kg on the exact fare family before paying.

Baggage & total-cost warning

Checked baggage, cabin baggage, seat selection, meals and change/refund rules depend on the airline and the exact fare family. Always confirm the allowance in kg/pieces on the airline or provider page for the fare you are about to buy. Compare the final payable total, not a base fare example. Any price example used while learning the tool is only an example of how to read the screen — it is not a live fare and is not a quote for this route.

Transit & booking-condition check

For connections, check layover, single-ticket vs separate-ticket risk, and UAE/transit entry rules on official sources before booking.

FAQ

What is the Dhaka to Dubai ticket price today?

It depends on your travel dates, airline and fare family, so we do not print a fixed price here. Open the search tool, pick your dates, and Google Flights will show today's real total.

Does Cheap Ticket Finder sell tickets?

No. We build the dated search; booking and payment happen with the airline/provider.

How can I lower the total cost?

Stay flexible with dates, compare the total including baggage, and check both non-stop and connecting options for total time and cost. No specific day or booking window guarantees the lowest fare.
