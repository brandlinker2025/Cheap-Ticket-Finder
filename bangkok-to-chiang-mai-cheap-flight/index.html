Which airlines — official sites only

Thai AirAsia, Thai VietJet, Thai Lion Air and Nok Air are low-cost airlines flying Bangkok to Chiang Mai. Thai Airways and Bangkok Airways also fly Bangkok to Chiang Mai, so compare the Bangkok departure airport (BKK or DMK), baggage and the final total for your dates.

  • Thai AirAsia (FD) Low-cost: Mainly DMK, some domestic from BKK — check the airport on your flight. Official: AirAsia flights. From Bangladesh, an international card is the realistic payment path; Thai local counter/bank options are for people in Thailand. Official payment/support source.
  • Nok Air (DD) Low-cost: DMK-based. Official booking/payment guide: Nok Air — how to book and pay (cards and other options listed there; counter/ATM requires being in Thailand).
  • Thai Lion Air (SL) Low-cost: DMK-based. Official: lionairthai.com. Foreign-card success was Unknown in our research — confirm at checkout only.
  • VietJet Thailand (VZ) Low-cost: BKK-based, which may avoid an airport change after arriving from Dhaka. Official: vietjetthai.com; group payment methods (verify on the Thai site before paying): VietJet payment methods.

Airport warning

BKK and DMK are different airports. BKK (Suvarnabhumi) is where Dhaka flights normally land; DMK (Don Mueang) is across the city. AirAsia runs Thai domestic services from both airports, Nok Air and Thai Lion Air are DMK-based in our research, and VietJet Thailand is BKK-based. A separate-ticket BKK arrival followed by a DMK departure means crossing Bangkok — independent guidance in our research suggests at least 3.5–4 hours, and a BKK departure or overnight stay is safer. That is cautious advice, not an official rule. Source: AirAsia newsroom — both Bangkok airports.

Separate-ticket & transit/entry caution

Two tickets = two contracts. If the first flight is late, the second airline is not obliged to rebook you free and the second fare can be lost as a no-show. With checked bags on separate tickets you may need immigration → baggage reclaim → check-in again, so keep a large buffer; arriving the night before the domestic leg is our safer advice (guidance, not an official airline rule).

Separate tickets / BKK↔DMK changes can mean entering Thailand. Do not assume airside transit or any entry eligibility on a separate ticket.

No verified official Thai entry link is provided in our research for this page, so none is guessed here. Check official Thai immigration/government sources and the airline for your passport and itinerary before you book.

Spell the passenger name exactly as in the passport — low-cost airlines can charge for name correction or refuse boarding (the amount varies, so we do not print a fee here). Cheap low-cost fares are often non-refundable and change fees can be high — read the fare rules before paying. Keep your booking confirmation and PNR safely with you; never send us passport or card images.
