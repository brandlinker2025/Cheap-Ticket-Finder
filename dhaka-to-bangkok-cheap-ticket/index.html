Dhaka to Bangkok Cheap Ticket

DAC → BKK / DMK · ঢাকা টু ব্যাংকক — how to check the cheapest real fare for your dates. Last updated: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). No live fare is published on this page.

Bangkok is one of the most searched routes from Dhaka. Prices change by date, airline and airport (Suvarnabhumi BKK or Don Mueang DMK), so this page does not print a live fare. Use the steps below to check today's real total price for your own dates.

How to find the cheapest dates (3 steps)

  1. Open the Cheap Ticket Finder search tool, set From / To to this route and pick flexible dates on the calendar.
  2. Use the primary Open Google Flights button. Google Flights / the airline there shows the real, current total price — that is where booking happens.
  3. Before paying, compare the total with baggage, seat and payment fees added. The lowest headline fare is not always the lowest total.

Route tips — Dhaka to Bangkok

  • Compare both Bangkok airports: a cheaper fare into DMK can cost more in time and transfers — compare the full trip, not only the ticket.
  • Try shifting departure or return by a few days in the calendar; weekday and off-peak dates are often priced differently, but no day is guaranteed cheapest.
  • If a one-stop option looks cheaper, check the layover length and whether baggage is included on both legs before deciding.

Baggage & total-cost warning

Checked baggage, cabin baggage, seat selection, meals and change/refund rules depend on the airline and the exact fare family. Always confirm the allowance in kg/pieces on the airline or provider page for the fare you are about to buy. Compare the final payable total, not a base fare example. Any price example used while learning the tool is only an example of how to read the screen — it is not a live fare and is not a quote for this route.

Transit & booking-condition check

Non-stop and connecting options may appear on Google Flights depending on date. For any connection, check layover time, whether you stay on one ticket, and transit-visa rules on official sources before booking.
