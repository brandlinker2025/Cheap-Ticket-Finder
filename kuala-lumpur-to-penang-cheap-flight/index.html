Airport warning

KUL and SZB are different airports. KUL is Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA, Terminals 1 and 2). SZB is Subang, a different airport across the city — and some Penang services, especially Firefly turboprop services, use Subang. International flights from Dhaka land at KUL, not SZB. Malaysia Aviation Group announced Firefly jet operations moved to KLIA from August 2025 while turboprop services stay at Subang, so read the airport code on the ticket itself. Source: Malaysia Aviation Group — Firefly jet operations.

Separate-ticket & transit/entry caution

Two tickets = two contracts. If the first flight is late, the second airline is not obliged to rebook you free and the second fare can be lost as a no-show. With checked bags on separate tickets you may need immigration → baggage reclaim → check-in again, so keep a large buffer; arriving the night before the domestic leg is our safer advice (guidance, not an official airline rule).

Separate tickets mean entering Malaysia. A separate Dhaka → Kuala Lumpur ticket plus this domestic leg normally means immigration, baggage reclaim and a new check-in at Kuala Lumpur — entering Malaysia, not an airside transit. Do not assume any transit-pass or visa eligibility.

Verify your case only on official sources: Malaysian High Commission in Dhaka and the official Malaysia Visa portal (terms).

Spell the passenger name exactly as in the passport — low-cost airlines can charge for name correction or refuse boarding (the amount varies, so we do not print a fee here). Cheap low-cost fares are often non-refundable and change fees can be high — read the fare rules before paying.
