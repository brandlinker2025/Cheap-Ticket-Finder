Search hotels, tours & activities on Klook

How it works (3 steps)

  1. Click the Klook button above. You leave Cheap Ticket Finder and open the partner site in a new tab.
  2. On the partner site, enter your destination, check-in / check-out dates, guests and rooms, then compare the options yourself.
  3. Book and pay on the partner site only. We do not take payment, and we never ask for passport, card or booking-login details.

Before you pay — room & total-cost checks

  • Compare the final payable total, including taxes and fees — not only the first nightly price you see.
  • Check room type, bed type, maximum guests, breakfast inclusion, and whether the rate is refundable or free-cancellation.
  • Confirm check-in / check-out times, property location and any extra charges (resort fee, deposit, extra bed) on the partner page.
  • For a flight + stay trip, keep your flight baggage and arrival time in mind when choosing check-in time — a late arrival can affect first-night check-in.

How to choose a stay

Start from the city you will actually sleep in, not only the airport code on your ticket. A hotel near the airport can shorten a late arrival. A hotel in the city can shorten the next day’s plans. Read the property address on the partner page and compare it with where you need to be.

Match the stay to the ticket. If the flight lands late, check the hotel’s check-in window and whether a late arrival needs a message to the property. If the ticket can change, read the cancellation deadline on the rate you select. Refundable and non-refundable rates are stated on the partner page.

Klook lists hotels together with tours and activities. Use the stay filters on Klook so you are looking at accommodation, then read the room, the guest count, and what is included before you pay. Cheap Ticket Finder does not hold rooms and does not add a charge of its own.
