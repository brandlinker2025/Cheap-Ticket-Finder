All Seats, All Flights

The Newsroom notice says AirAsia is offering up to 30% off flights across its network in the All Seats, All Flights sale. The asterisk on that discount means one-way travel, base fare only. It is not inclusive of passenger service charge, regulatory service charges, fuel surcharges, and other applicable fees. Taxes and fees are extra.

  • Booking: now until 25 October 2026.
  • Travel: 2 November 2026 to 30 September 2027.
  • Discount: up to 30% off the one-way base fare. Not a fixed fare, and not a promise that every seat is 30% off.

The notice names these featured routes from Kuala Lumpur: Penang, Manila, Palembang, Phu Quoc, Kaohsiung, Tokyo, Melbourne, Beijing, Istanbul, Almaty, and more. That list is what the Newsroom published. It is not a timetable, and it does not say these cities are served from Dhaka.

The same notice does not name Dhaka. This page does not claim an AirAsia flight to or from Dhaka. Search your own route and dates, then read the total on the provider before you pay.

What to check before you pay

The percentage is on the base fare. The amount you pay also includes the charges the notice lists as extra. Compare the final payable total on the booking site, including bags if you need them. A headline discount is not the amount charged to your card.

Offers change. If the booking window has passed, or the travel dates on the provider do not match the notice, the sale may not apply. Confirm the dates on the official notice and on the booking page. Cheap Ticket Finder does not hold seats and does not take payment.
