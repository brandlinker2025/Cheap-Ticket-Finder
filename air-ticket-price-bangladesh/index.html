How to pay less

  1. Search a few days before and after your date — the cheapest day can be much lower.
  2. Compare the total with baggage, not the first price you see.
  3. Open the airline's own website for the same flight; book where the total is lower.
  4. Check bank-card offers (for example EBL, City Amex) on Bangladeshi booking sites.
  5. For long trips, compare starting from Kolkata, Delhi, Kathmandu or Colombo.

Frequently asked questions

What is the air ticket price from Dhaka to Dubai?

On 4 November 2026 the cheapest one-way fare we saw for 10 December 2026 was US$296 from Dhaka and US$245 from Kolkata. Prices change daily — use the search for today's fare.

Which airline is cheapest from Bangladesh?

No single airline is always cheapest. Low-cost carriers such as AirAsia, IndiGo and flydubai are often lower before baggage; compare the total with baggage for your date.

Is it cheaper to buy a ticket from another country?

Changing only the website's country made no difference in our check. Starting the flight in a nearby country such as India was often cheaper.
