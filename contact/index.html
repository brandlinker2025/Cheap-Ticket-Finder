What to include

  • The route and dates you searched (for example DAC → BKK).
  • Which link looked wrong, expired, or unclear, and what the provider showed.
  • A screenshot only if it does not contain personal or payment details.

What we cannot do

We cannot book, change, cancel, or refund a ticket for you — only the airline or provider you booked with can do that. Contact them directly for booking changes.
